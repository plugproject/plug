---
title: Pattern catalog
---

The catalog contains direct request, cache-aside, scheduled refresh/prefetch + cache,
progressive useful output + live completion, scheduled cache with miss filling, and
cached initial output followed by live refresh. General composition is deferred.

Cache requires an eligible read, explicit permission, complete scoped keys and
accepted indicative age. Only enumerated keys are retained; other keys do not evict
hot entries. Calls coalesce per key. Failed refreshes never advance timestamps.

For start-to-start cadence I, bounded duration D, scheduling allowance J, source lag L,
and delivery allowance U, require I + D + J + L + U <= maxAge. Reads reserve U too.
Candidate intervals are 1, 5, 10, 15, 30, 60, 120, 300 and 600 seconds. Prefer the
largest feasible interval, optionally at most half the typical change interval as a
soft heuristic. Per-key cyclic offsets are checked for overlap, capacity and quota.

Replay starts cold with visible scheduled prewarm work, uses the exact policy, counts
expiry/refill gaps and unenumerated keys, and assumes successful bounded source calls.
Absent matching observed policy evidence or a supplied trace, coverage is unknown.
Continuous replay work is capped; oversized simulation needs better bounded evidence.
Runtime failures and restarts can reduce replay coverage and require revalidation.
