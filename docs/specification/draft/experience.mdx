---
title: Required experience
---

The author specifies useful output, latency target, accepted age, correctness,
latency scope, indicative-result permission, deferred-result permission, final
deadline and allowed miss behavior. Latency scope is warmHits, trafficPercentile
or everyRequest. Cache hits cannot certify every request because cold/expired
states exist. A live requirement cannot be silently weakened to indicative output.

Traffic success is bounded by usable hits times independently evidenced within-target
hit probability when misses are slow. Summing point estimates or percentiles does
not establish that probability. Missing evidence remains a conditional claim.
